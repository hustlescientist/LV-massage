Your recovery journey

Different phases need different support.

There is no single recovery timeline. Early care may focus on swelling, comfort, and gentle lymphatic support. Later care can shift toward tissue mobility, tightness, scar support, compression, and returning to normal movement.

  1. 01

    Early recovery

    Education, swelling support, comfort, gentle movement, and compression awareness.

  2. 02

    Continued healing

    Changing tissue, mobility, tightness, scar development, and evolving garment needs.

  3. 03

    Later recovery

    Fibrosis and scar support, mobility work, and longer-term recovery goals.

Meet Sabrina Gonzalez

Recovery expertise built through hands-on care and continuing education.

Sabrina became a Licensed Massage Therapist in 2016 and continued training in manual lymphatic drainage, breast massage, cosmetic surgery therapy, and myofascial release. Her work increasingly focused on post-operative recovery as she saw how much clients benefit from providers who understand lymphatics, scar tissue, fascia, compression, and changing recovery needs.

Las Vegas Cosmetic Recovery is also family-centered. Sabrina credits her mother with helping operate the practice behind the scenes and her daughter with being a constant source of inspiration.
